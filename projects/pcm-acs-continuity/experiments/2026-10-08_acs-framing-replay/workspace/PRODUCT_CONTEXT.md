# Sourced product context (bounded excerpt)

This is a **reconstructed** internal context packet for a lab exercise, not an owner-approved current marketing brief. Its claims are bounded by the upstream repositories and linked sources. The task's target story, audience and acceptance belong to the *live owning issue*.

**PCM — Project Continuity Modules.** A GitHub-first project-continuity CLI and contract for long-horizon tasks, checkpoints, resumed handoffs, evidence links, request-ID retry safety, PR/CI progression and authority reconciliation. It is not a Temporal-compatible event engine and cannot by itself certify the product meaning of a drafted page. Source: https://github.com/Pukujan/project-continuity-modules.

**ACS — Agent Custom Setup.** An install/hotload and multi-agent coordination stack with decision-boss lease, join-order roles, a GitHub-oriented claim queue, agent-less watchdog and propose-to-PR workflow. A seat/lease does not establish factual truth or an authorized new marketing position. Source: https://github.com/Pukujan/agent-custom-setup.

**OIO — Observational Issue Ops.** Shared filing ontology for observational and operational issue evidence with filer provenance and triage; filing observations is not implementation authorization. Source: https://github.com/Pukujan/observational-issue-ops.

**CGM — Content Generation Modules.** Writing/context/brand, human readability, asset naming, visual direction, images and HTML assistance. It is not a product-research or buyer-insight guarantee. Source: https://github.com/Pukujan/content-generation-modules.

All four are related but have distinct ownership. Do not make up features, deployment guarantees, buyer quotes, audience research, or named personas. Do not copy files from a real production adopter site.
