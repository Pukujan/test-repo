# FOSSIL Experiment Rules

Read root `AGENTS.md` first.

For FOSSIL experiments:

- Treat `Pukujan/fossil-core` architecture/contracts as upstream authority; this lab records evidence, not architecture truth.
- Verify the exact upstream SHA before every run.
- Preserve source fidelity and capture completeness as separate fields.
- Do not claim a shared-chat capture is complete unless traversal/accounting proves it.
- Do not silently upgrade reconstructed evidence to verbatim.
- Do not rewrite prior raw captures; a fuller later capture is additive evidence.
- Retrieval/index/projection outputs are rebuildable test outputs, not canonical truth.
- When a defect is found, link the focused `fossil-core` issue and keep the experiment checklist synchronized.
